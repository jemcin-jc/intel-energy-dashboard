# Intel Energy Dashboard

## Overview

This project analyzes U.S. regional energy production using Tableau to determine the best location for a future Intel data center. The dashboard compares net production, renewable energy generation, and regional energy sources to support data-driven decision making.

---

## Project Objectives

- Compare net energy production across regions
- Analyze renewable energy generation
- Explore energy generation by source
- Support business decisions with interactive visualizations

---

## Dashboard Features

- Interactive Region filter
- Day, Week, and Month parameter
- Net Production analysis
- Renewable Energy Percentage by Region
- Energy Source by Region

---

## Tools Used

- Tableau
- Data Visualization
- Dashboard Design
- Calculated Fields
- Parameters
- Filters

---

## Business Recommendation

Based on my dashboard, I recommend the Northwest (NW) region for Intel's next data center. While the Mid Atlantic region has the largest energy surplus, the Northwest combines positive net production with the highest percentage of renewable energy, making it the strongest long-term choice for balancing energy reliability and sustainability.
